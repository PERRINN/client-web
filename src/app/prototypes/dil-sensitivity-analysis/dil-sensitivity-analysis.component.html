Value for a motorsport team

Reuse a representative lap to screen setup ideas, isolate parameter effects and turn telemetry into a traceable test plan. Reduce repeated manual comparisons and focus simulator time on promising directions.

This demonstrator illustrates the workflow. It does not quantify time savings, prove lap gains or recommend a real setup.
